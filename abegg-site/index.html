Questions people ask

How does ABEGG keep me anonymous?

Members only see your request and a one-time alias. Your name, photo, phone number and location are never shown to anyone.

Can I speak my request instead of typing?

Yes. Record a voice note of up to a minute, or type it. Members can reply to either.

What happens after someone replies?

Their reply shows up in your request thread. You decide who to keep talking to and who to ignore.

Who can see my request?

Only ABEGG members. Requests are not posted on public pages.
